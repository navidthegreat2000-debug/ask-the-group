From: Maria Lopez
To: Volunteers
Subject: Saturday setup — full details

Hi everyone,

Just a reminder for this Saturday.

SETUP
Setup starts at 8am in the hall. If you're on the early slot, use the
side entrance — the front doors are locked until 8:30.

PARKING
On normal Saturdays, use the school car park round the back. On match
days it fills up early, so use the church car park instead — it's about
a 3 minute walk.

WHAT TO WEAR
Comfortable clothes. Closed-toe shoes in the kitchen (safety). Aprons
are provided — spares are in the cupboard by the sink.

IF YOU CAN'T MAKE IT
Text the group number as early as you can so we can find cover. The
number is 555-0199. Please don't email — I don't check it on Saturdays.

KITCHEN
The kitchen opens at 10am, and we start serving at 10:30. Kitchen
shifts are 3 hours. Food pantry shifts are 2 hours. Setup is 1 hour.

CERTIFICATES
Kitchen volunteers need a food hygiene certificate. Pantry volunteers
don't. If you need one, we can help you book the free online course
through the council.

Thanks,
Maria
