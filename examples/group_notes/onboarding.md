# New Volunteer Guide

Welcome! Here's what you need to know before your first shift.

## When is my shift?

Shifts are listed on the shared rota, which is sent out monthly.

- **Setup shift:** 1 hour, starts at 8am
- **Food pantry shift:** 2 hours
- **Kitchen shift:** 3 hours, opens at 10am, serving starts 10:30

## Where do I go?

Come to the hall. On setup, use the side entrance — the front doors
are locked until 8:30.

## Where do I park?

- **Normal Saturdays:** school car park, round the back
- **Match days:** church car park — the school one fills up early

## What do I wear?

Comfortable clothes. If you're on kitchen, closed-toe shoes are
required (safety). Aprons are provided — spares are in the cupboard
by the sink.

## Do I need a certificate?

- **Kitchen:** yes, a food hygiene certificate. It's free for
  volunteers — we can help you book the online course through the
  council.
- **Food pantry:** no certificate needed.

## What if I can't make it?

Text the group number as early as you can so we can find cover.
The number is **555-0199**. Please don't email — it isn't checked
on Saturdays.

## Can I bring my child?

Children over 12 can help in the food pantry with a parent. The
kitchen is adults only, for safety reasons.

## What if a client gets upset?

Stay calm, don't argue, and get Maria or Ana. There's a one-page
policy in the folder — worth reading before your first shift.

## First aid

The first aid kit is on the kitchen wall, next to the fire
extinguisher. Both get checked monthly. If something's missing,
tell a coordinator.

## Training

There's a training day on the first Saturday of every month at 9am.
Not mandatory, but useful for new volunteers.
